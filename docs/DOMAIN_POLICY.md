# SORCE Domain Management Policy

_Last updated: February 2026_

---

## 1. Domain Ownership & Registration

Domains purchased through SORCE are registered by SORCE on your behalf using our domain registrar account. While you have full operational control of your domain (DNS, routing, email configuration), the domain is held under SORCE's registrar account.

You retain the right to transfer your domain to any registrar of your choosing at any time. SORCE will not withhold, sell, or transfer your domain to any third party without your explicit written consent.

---

## 2. Domain Renewals

Domains purchased through SORCE are registered for one year and renewed automatically before expiration, provided your SORCE subscription is active and in good standing.

- You will receive renewal reminder emails **60 days**, **30 days**, and **7 days** before expiration
- Renewal fees will be charged to the payment method on file for your SORCE subscription
- If renewal payment fails, SORCE will attempt to charge your payment method up to **3 times** over 7 days before the domain is placed in a grace period
- Domains not renewed within the registrar's grace period may be released and become available for public registration — SORCE is not responsible for domain loss due to failed payment

---

## 3. Cancellation & Account Termination

If you cancel your SORCE subscription:

- Your domain will remain registered and functional for the remainder of its current registration period
- **Within 30 days of cancellation**, you may request a free domain transfer to your own registrar account — SORCE will provide the EPP/auth transfer code within 3 business days of request
- After 30 days, SORCE may allow the domain to expire at the end of its registration period if no transfer has been initiated
- SORCE will send transfer reminder emails at cancellation, 15 days, and 28 days post-cancellation

---

## 4. Domain Transfer Out

To transfer your domain away from SORCE at any time:

1. Submit a transfer request via your dashboard or by emailing support
2. SORCE will unlock the domain and provide your EPP/auth code within **3 business days**
3. Initiate the transfer through your chosen registrar — standard ICANN transfer rules apply (60-day lock after registration or recent transfer)
4. There is no transfer fee charged by SORCE

> **Note:** Transferring your domain away will disconnect it from your SORCE-hosted website unless you manually update your DNS records.

---

## 5. Service Interruptions

SORCE maintains its domain registrar account with reasonable care, including automatic account balance top-ups to prevent purchase or renewal failures. In the event of a registrar-side outage or failed renewal due to circumstances beyond SORCE's control:

- SORCE will notify affected users within 24 hours
- SORCE will make commercially reasonable efforts to restore domain function within 48 hours
- SORCE's liability for domain-related losses is limited to the amount paid for the domain registration

---

## 6. Prohibited Uses

Domains registered through SORCE may not be used for spam, phishing, illegal activity, or any use that violates SORCE's general Terms of Service. SORCE reserves the right to suspend a domain in response to abuse complaints or legal orders, with notice to the domain owner where legally permitted.
