Common questions

What buyers ask us.

How long does tint installation take?+

Most full vehicles are 2–4 hours in our shop. Sedans on the lower end, SUVs and trucks toward the higher end. You're welcome to wait or drop off.

When can I roll my windows down after?+

Wait 3–5 days before rolling the windows down. The film needs to fully bond to the glass — rolling early can cause edges to lift. We'll tell you the exact timeframe based on weather.

Will tint look bubbly or hazy at first?+

Yes — for a few days. That's water trapped between the film and glass during install, and it's totally normal. It dries out fully within 1–2 weeks and the film becomes crystal clear.

What's the WA state tint law?+

Washington requires front side windows to allow at least 24% visible light. Rear side windows and back glass have no VLT limit on private passenger vehicles. We'll always quote a legal combination by default.

Do you offer a warranty?+

Yes — lifetime warranty against bubbling, peeling, cracking, and color fade on our carbon and ceramic films. The dyed film carries a 5-year warranty.

Will tint affect my back-window defroster?+

No. Our films are non-conductive and install over the defroster lines without affecting them. They also won't interfere with GPS, cell signal, or remote start.
