Common questions

What buyers ask us.

Do you install commercial fleet wraps near Olympia, WA?+

Yes — we design and install commercial fleet wraps for businesses in and around Olympia, WA, from a single work truck to a full fleet. We can also handle partial wraps and decal-only branding if a full wrap isn't in the budget.

What's the difference between a full wrap, partial wrap, and decal application?+

A full wrap covers every exterior panel for maximum visibility. A partial wrap covers the sides and rear — the panels people actually see in traffic and parking lots — while the hood and roof stay factory. Decal application is cut vinyl graphics (your logo, name and number) applied directly, without covering the painted panels. All three are real options depending on budget and coverage.

How much does a vehicle wrap cost?+

It depends on the vehicle size, the coverage (full, partial or decal), and the finish. We'll give you an exact quote once we know your vehicle and what you're picturing — request a free mockup above and we'll follow up with pricing.

Will a wrap damage my vehicle's factory paint?+

No — a properly installed and removed wrap doesn't harm paint in good condition. It's designed to protect the paint underneath while it's on, and to come off clean.

Do you offer chrome delete near me?+

Yes — we install precision-cut black chrome delete film over window trim, grille surrounds, mirror caps and badges, in gloss, satin or matte black.
