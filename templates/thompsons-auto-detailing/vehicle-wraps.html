Why wrap

A new look today. Your factory paint underneath, untouched.

A wrap is precision-cut vinyl applied over your factory paint — not a repaint. That means no sanding, no chemical prep, no permanent change, and a finish that comes off clean whenever you're ready for something new or ready to sell.

It's also protection: the vinyl becomes a sacrificial layer between your paint and the road, catching the light chips and scuffs that would otherwise hit the paint itself.

  • Any color, any finish — gloss, matte, satin, chrome or color-shift
  • Fully reversible — your original paint is preserved underneath
  • Shields factory paint from minor chips, scuffs and UV fade
  • Custom graphics, liveries and logos are all doable, not just color
  • Installed in days, not the weeks a full repaint takes

Wrap vs. repaint

Why most people wrap instead of repainting.

A repaint is permanent and it's the only way to fix paint that's already damaged. For everything else — a new look, added protection, or getting ready to sell — a wrap wins on cost, time and reversibility.

Repaint

Permanent, and the right call for damaged paint.

  • Sanding and chemical prep down to bare metal
  • Typically weeks in the shop, not days
  • Permanent — can't be undone if you change your mind
  • No protective layer over the new paint
  • Higher cost for full-vehicle color changes

A new look with your factory paint kept safe underneath.

  • Most vehicles done in a few days
  • Fully reversible — remove it anytime, paint intact
  • Vinyl itself shields paint from minor chips and UV
  • Lower cost, especially for a full color change

Common questions

What buyers ask us.

Will a wrap damage my factory paint?+

No — installed and removed correctly, a wrap doesn't harm paint in good condition. It's designed to come off clean, leaving the paint underneath exactly as it was. If your paint is already damaged or has been repainted with lower-quality paint, we'll check it first and let you know what to expect.

How long does a wrap last?+

Most quality vinyl wraps hold up well for several years with normal care, depending on the finish, your climate, and how the vehicle is parked and washed. We'll go over care and expected lifespan for the specific vinyl you choose.

Can I wash and drive it normally?+

Yes. Hand washing or a touchless car wash is best — avoid brush-style automatic washes and pressure washing directly at panel edges. Otherwise it drives, parks and washes like normal paint.

Can you match any color, or a specific design?+

Yes — vinyl comes in an enormous range of factory colors and finishes, and we can also work from a reference color or design you bring us. For custom graphics or a full livery, we'll design it with you before anything is cut.

How long does the install take?+

A full color change typically takes a few days; spot graphics or a partial wrap can often be done same day. We'll give you an exact timeline once we know your vehicle and the scope.

Can I remove it myself before selling the car?+

We recommend having it removed professionally so nothing is left behind and the paint underneath is checked and protected during removal — but yes, that's exactly the point of a wrap: it comes off, and your original paint is what's underneath.
