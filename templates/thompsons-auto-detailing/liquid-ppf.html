What it is

PPF chemistry. Acrylic strength. Sprayed-on finish.

NANOPRO Liquid PPF shares the polymer backbone of traditional paint protection film, but adds an acrylic reinforcement that gives it noticeably stronger impact resistance than the original.

Because it's applied as a liquid that self-levels and cures, there are no seams, edges, or lifting points — it bonds to the entire painted surface as one continuous shell.

  • Acrylic-reinforced — stronger impact & abrasion resistance than traditional PPF
  • Seamless application — no edges to lift, no visible film lines
  • Hydrophobic finish — water beads and rolls off, dirt sticks less
  • Deep, glass-like gloss that enhances any factory paint color
  • UV-stable — won't yellow, fade, or chalk over time

NANOPRO vs. traditional PPF.

Same protective DNA. Different physical and aesthetic profile.

Traditional PPF

Pre-cut polyurethane film, hand-applied panel by panel.

  • Strong impact protection from rocks & debris
  • Visible edges where film stops on each panel
  • Seams can collect dirt or lift over time
  • Limited self-healing on minor swirl marks
  • Standard PPF gloss profile

NANOPRO Liquid PPF

Same chemistry, reinforced with acrylic, sprayed-on for full coverage.

  • Stronger impact resistance — acrylic-reinforced layer
  • Seamless, edge-free coverage across the whole car
  • Bonds as one continuous shell — nothing to lift
  • Hydrophobic finish that sheds water and dirt
  • Deeper, glass-like gloss enhancement

Common questions

What buyers ask us.

Is NANOPRO actually stronger than traditional PPF?+

It's the same polymer chemistry as traditional PPF, but reinforced with acrylic. The acrylic component improves impact and abrasion resistance compared to standard PPF. Where it really pulls ahead is application — there are no seams or edges where damage can start.

Should I get NANOPRO or a ceramic coating?+

Ceramic coating is purely a chemical-resistant gloss layer — it doesn't stop rock chips. NANOPRO does both: it gives you the gloss and hydrophobic properties of ceramic, plus the impact protection of PPF. If your priority is preventing physical damage to your paint, NANOPRO is the upgrade.

How long does the application take?+

A full vehicle is a 2-day appointment in our climate-controlled studio. Day 1 is wash, decon, and paint correction. Day 2 is prep, application, and overnight cure. You drop off, we call when it's ready.

When can I wash my car after?+

Wait 7 days before washing, and avoid automatic car washes for the first 30. Hand wash with a pH-neutral soap is fine after the first week. We'll give you a written care sheet at pickup.

Will it damage my factory paint?+

No. NANOPRO bonds on top of your clear coat — it doesn't penetrate the paint. If you ever wanted to remove it, it can be polished off. It actually protects the original paint from UV fade and chemical etching.

Can NANOPRO go over an existing ceramic coating?+

Not directly. The bond requires a chemically clean surface, so we'll strip and correct any existing coating first as part of the prep stage. That's included in the price.
