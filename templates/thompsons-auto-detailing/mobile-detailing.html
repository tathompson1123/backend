Common questions

What buyers ask us.

How long does a full detail take?+

Express is 1.5–2 hours. Signature is 4–6 hours. Restoration runs 6–10 hours depending on the condition. We'll give you an honest estimate when you book.

Do I need to be there for mobile service?+

Not the whole time. We just need access to the car and a place to set up. Most clients leave the keys with us and run errands while we work.

Do you bring your own water and power?+

Yes. Our mobile rig is fully self-contained — water tank, generator, lights, all the supplies. We don't need anything from your house.

What if it's raining the day of my mobile appointment?+

Light rain is fine — we work under canopies. Heavy weather, we'll reach out the day before to reschedule or move you to our shop. No charges for weather reschedules.

Can you remove pet hair, stains, or odors?+

Yes. Heavy pet hair, set-in stains, and persistent odors are part of the Restoration package. Tell us what you're dealing with when you book and we'll quote accordingly.

What's your guarantee?+

100% money-back. If you don't love how it looks when we hand the keys back, you don't pay. We've never had to honor it — and we don't plan to.
