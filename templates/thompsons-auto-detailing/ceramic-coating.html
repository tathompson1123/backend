What it is

A liquid polymer that becomes part of your paint.

A ceramic coating is a nano-engineered liquid that bonds chemically with your clear coat. Once cured, it forms a hard, transparent layer that's far more durable than wax — and far glossier.

You stop waxing. You wash less. Water sheets off. Contaminants don't bond. The shine stays for years instead of weeks.

  • Bonds chemically to paint — not a surface layer like wax
  • Hydrophobic — water beads up and rolls off
  • UV stable — protects against fade and oxidation
  • Chemical resistant — bird droppings, sap, road salt all wipe off
  • Backed by a 5-year manufacturer warranty
Why upgrade

Ceramic vs. wax & sealants.

If you're still waxing every season, here's what you're missing.

Wax / Sealant

A surface-level layer that sits on top of the paint.

  • Lasts 6 weeks to 6 months
  • Sits on top of clear coat, doesn't bond
  • Needs reapplication every season
  • Soft — washes off in heavy rain or detergents
  • Mild gloss, mild water repellency

Professional Ceramic Coating

A nano-polymer that chemically bonds to your clear coat.

  • Lasts 3–5+ years with one application
  • Bonds chemically — becomes part of the surface
  • One install, multi-year protection
  • Hard, durable shell — resists detergent & UV
  • Deep, glass-like gloss and aggressive water beading

Our process

How a ceramic coat gets done right.

The coating only locks in what it can see. Prep is everything.

1

Decontamination wash

Foam wash, iron remover, clay bar treatment. Every embedded contaminant pulled out before correction.

2

Paint correction

Multi-stage machine polishing to remove swirl marks, scratches, and oxidation. Whatever's there when we coat is what gets locked in for years.

3

Surface prep

IPA wipedown to strip every trace of polishing oil. The ceramic needs a chemically clean surface to bond.

4

Coat & cure

Hand-applied in panels, leveled, and cured in our climate-controlled studio. You pick up a finished car the next day.

Common questions

What buyers ask us.

How long does a ceramic coating last?+

Our 2-year coating is good for ~24 months of full performance. The 5-year coating, with proper maintenance, lasts 5+ years. Either way, you stop waxing.

Will ceramic coating prevent rock chips?+

No — ceramic is a chemical-resistance and gloss layer, not impact protection. If rock chips are your concern, look at NANOPRO Liquid PPF, which protects against both chemical and physical damage.

Can you coat over my existing paint?+

Yes — that's actually ideal. Whatever condition your paint is in, we'll correct it first to remove swirl marks and scratches, then lock in the corrected finish under the coating.

Do I still need to wash my car?+

Yes, but less often, and the washes go faster. We'll send you a written maintenance guide — pH-neutral soap, two-bucket method, no automatic brush washes.

When can I drive my car after coating?+

Same-day pickup is fine — the coating is dry to the touch in hours. Avoid washing for 7 days, and avoid heavy rain or detergent for the first 24 hours.

Is there a warranty?+

Yes — manufacturer warranty against premature failure (2 or 5 years depending on package), plus our 100% money-back guarantee on the install itself.
