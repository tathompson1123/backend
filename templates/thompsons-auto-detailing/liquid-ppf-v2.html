What it is

PPF chemistry. Acrylic strength. Seamless finish.

NANOPRO Liquid PPF shares the polymer backbone of traditional paint protection film, but adds an acrylic reinforcement that gives it noticeably stronger impact and abrasion resistance than the original.

Because it's applied as a liquid that self-levels and cures, there are no seams, edges, or lifting points — it bonds to the entire painted surface as one continuous shell with no visible lines.

  • Acrylic-reinforced — stronger impact & abrasion resistance than traditional PPF
  • Seamless application — no edges to lift, no visible film lines
  • Hydrophobic finish — water beads and rolls off, dirt sticks less
  • Deep, glass-like gloss that enhances any factory paint color
  • UV-stable — won't yellow, fade, or chalk over time

Common questions

What buyers ask us.

Is NANOPRO actually stronger than traditional PPF?+

It's the same polymer chemistry as traditional PPF, but reinforced with acrylic for improved impact and abrasion resistance. Where it really pulls ahead is the application — it goes on as a liquid and self-levels into one continuous shell, so there are no seams or edges where damage and lifting usually start.

How thick is the NANOPRO layer?+

Far thicker than a ceramic coating, which is only molecules deep. NANOPRO builds a real, acrylic-reinforced sacrificial layer over your clear coat — substantial enough to absorb chips and abrasion — while self-leveling so there are no visible lines.

What does the 10-year warranty and "free fixes for life" cover?+

The 10-year warranty covers the coating against yellowing, cracking, and delamination. On top of that, if you ever pick up a chip or scratch in the coating, we'll re-treat the affected area free for as long as you own the vehicle. You get a written copy of both at pickup.

Should I get NANOPRO or a ceramic coating?+

Ceramic coating is purely a chemical-resistant gloss layer — it doesn't stop rock chips. NANOPRO does both: it gives you the gloss and hydrophobic properties of ceramic, plus the impact protection of PPF. If your priority is preventing physical damage to your paint, NANOPRO is the upgrade.

How long does the application take?+

A full vehicle is a 2-day appointment in our climate-controlled studio. Day 1 is wash, decon, and paint correction. Day 2 is prep, application, and overnight cure. You drop off, we call when it's ready.

When can I wash my car after?+

Wait 7 days before washing, and avoid automatic car washes for the first 30. Hand wash with a pH-neutral soap is fine after the first week. We'll give you a written care sheet at pickup.

Will it damage my factory paint?+

No. NANOPRO bonds on top of your clear coat — it doesn't penetrate the paint. If you ever wanted to remove it, it can be polished off. It actually protects the original paint from UV fade and chemical etching.
